* The load-bearing invariant (do not break this)

  d3 iterates its forces in *Map insertion order* and integrates =x += vx=
  after all of them run. The container spring pulls inward, so the packing
  force must be registered *last*: it is the one that clamps the velocity
  pointing into a gap. Register =fit= then =collide=. Swapping those two
  lines reproduces the overlap bug at ~5% of a field (measured).

  #+begin_example
  if (room) engine.d3Force('fit', forceFitContainer(room, extentOf))
  else engine.d3Force('fit', null)
  engine.d3Force('collide', forceLabelBoxes(extentOf, room?.blockers ?? []))
  #+end_example

  The reason the whole thing works at all: labels paint at =11 / globalScale=,
  so a title keeps its *screen* size while its *world* footprint is
  =labelWidth / k=. A collide reserve authored at zoom 1 is only honoured
  when the camera lands at k = 1. So the field must pack into the readable
  room, =fitReadable= computes ≥ 1, and the 1:1 cap binds. If you shrink the
  boxes, the camera zooms out, the titles grow back into each other.
