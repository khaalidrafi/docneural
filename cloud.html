// 01 · FOR HUMANS — THE READER

01. A HUD, NOT A MAGAZINE

DevDocs aggregates pages. docneural aggregates synapses: every cross-reference is a wire you can walk, with the citing sentence as provenance. Static CDN bytes — no runtime LLM cost, ever.

H-01

CITATION GRAPH

org-roam-style neuron canvas: ego graphs, co-citation, learn-paths. Every edge answers "why does this cite that?" with a sentence.

H-02

SECTION SEARCH

BM25 over a per-manual heading index, lazy-loaded. Ctrl+K palette, keyboard-first reading (j/k, g d/s/h, r, p).

H-03

CONTEXT PACKS

Tick chapters, get one markdown bundle with a token estimate — paste-ready fuel for any LLM chat.

H-04

BUILD-TIME TLDRs

One-line summaries per chapter, generated at build with YOUR key, cached by content hash. The runtime pays nothing.

H-05

READ ALOUD + PEEK

Speech synthesis for chapters; hover-peek cards on citations so you never lose your place.

H-06

YOUR CORPUS, YOUR DOMAIN

Cloud hosting: push any Org/texinfo manual family, get this exact site on your domain in minutes.

// 02 · COOL AI INTEGRATION — TYPESAFE JEV (SYSTEM ONE)

02. DECISIONS, NOT ESSAYS

Jev is TypeSafe's System-One model: send a state + typed questions, get typed answers with probabilities — choice / score / noul — in ~70 ms at ~$0.042 per 1M input tokens. The DOC ROUTER pack is what /v1/ask runs in production: route a question to the right manual, then judge candidates. Paste your own key (stays in your browser) or watch the offline SIM.

STATE // INPUT MODE: SIM

Live calls need http(s) + your key; a file:// preview runs the SIM. The SIM is deterministic and clearly labelled — it never pretends to be Jev.

ANSWERS // TYPED

Typed answers render here with probability bars and confidence. Nothing leaves your browser except the direct call to api.typesafe.ai when you supply a key.
