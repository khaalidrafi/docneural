* The Export Pipeline
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: The-Export-Pipeline
:END:

Previous: [[id:demo--install][Building the Site]]
Next: [[id:demo--agents][For Agents]]

Batch Emacs reads each manual, rewrites every =id:= link in the
parse tree — never with a regex, because Org examples are full of
links that must not become wires — and emits the graph plus body
fragments. Node then slims the transport: citation prose moves out
of =graph.json= into per-node files, and the result is precompressed.

* The nav header rule
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: The-nav-header-rule
:END:

Info manuals print =Next:=, =Previous:= and =Up:= headers. Those
paragraphs are structure, not citation: they become the reader's
chapter tree, and their links are skipped in the wire set. The rule
is region-based, so a header wrapped across lines is understood as
well as one printed on a single line.
