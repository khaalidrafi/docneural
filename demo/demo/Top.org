#+title: The docneural Manual

* The docneural Manual

Next: [[id:demo--intro][Introduction]]

Welcome to the demo corpus. It is small on purpose: seven files that
exercise every shape the exporter understands — nav headers, inner
cross-references, a wrapped header, and one Info stub pointer — so a
fresh clone can build a real site in seconds, and so you can see what
your own corpus will look like here.
