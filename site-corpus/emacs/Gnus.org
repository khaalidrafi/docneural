** 36 Email and Usenet News with Gnus [[#Email-and-Usenet-News-with-Gnus][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Email-and-Usenet-News-with-Gnus
:CLASS: chapter section-level-set-chapter
:END:
Gnus is an Emacs package primarily designed for reading and posting Usenet news. It can also be used to read and respond to messages from a number of other sources---email, remote directories, digests, and so on. Here we introduce Gnus and describe several basic features. For full details, see [[../gnus_html/index.html#Top][Gnus]] in The Gnus Manual.
