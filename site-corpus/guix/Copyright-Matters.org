*** 22.9 Copyright Matters [[#Copyright-Matters-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Copyright-Matters-1
:CLASS: section
:END:
GNU Guix is made available under the [[https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html][GNU GPL version 3 or later]] license, and authors retain their copyright. Below are some copyright-related project policies.

- [[#Copyright-Notices][Copyright Notices]]
- [[#Coding-Assistants-_0028LLM_0029-Policy][Coding Assistants (LLM) Policy]]

<<Copyright-Notices>>
**** 22.9.1 Copyright Notices [[#Copyright-Notices][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: copyright-notices
:CLASS: subsection
:END:
The copyright notices in Guix follow the guidance provided by the GNU Project (see [[https://www.gnu.org/prep/maintain/html_node/Copyright-Notices.html#Copyright-Notices][Copyright Notices]] in Information for Maintainers of GNU Software. Using ranges for adjacent years is preferred to listing each individual years. Below is an example of the preferred style used for copyright notices in source file headers:

#+begin_src lisp
;;; Copyright © 2019-2023, 2025 Your Name <email@example.org>
#+end_src

The above example means there were copyright-able changes made for the years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025.

<<Coding-Assistants-_0028LLM_0029-Policy>>
**** 22.9.2 Coding Assistants (LLM) Policy [[#Coding-Assistants-_0028LLM_0029-Policy][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: coding-assistants-llm-policy
:CLASS: subsection
:END:
We do not accept contributions that were written by, or based on code written by, a LLM (Large Language Model) coding assistant. This is due to the uncertain copyright status of the outputs generated by an LLM, which could be incompatible with and undermine the [[https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html][license]] used by the GNU Guix project.
