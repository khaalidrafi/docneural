*** 9.17 Extending Guix [[#Extending-Guix-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Extending-Guix-1
:CLASS: section
:END:
See [[id:guix--Utilities][Utilities]], for examples of commands that come with Guix.

One can introduce new Guix commands through the =GUIX_EXTENSIONS_PATH= variable. Guix will search that path for Guile modules which expose symbols defined through =define-command=.

Additionally, the command lookup machinery will search for extensions provided by channels which contain a =guix/extensions= subdirectory.

We encourage you to contribute your extensions to the [[https://codeberg.org/guix-extensions][Guix Extensions]] organization. The organization aims to gather community developed Guix extensions that are not ready to be merged into Guix proper or are too niche to be part of Guix.
