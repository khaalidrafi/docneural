**** 22.11.1 The Issue Tracker
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: the-issue-tracker
:CLASS: subsection
:END:
<<index-bug-reports_002c-tracking>> <<index-patch-submissions_002c-tracking>> <<index-issue-tracking>> <<index-Debbugs_002c-issue-tracking-system>>
Bug reports (/issues/) and patch submissions (/pull requests/) are currently tracked at the [[https://codeberg.org/guix/guix][project's home at Codeberg]].

Before May 25th, 2025, bug reports and patches were exclusively tracked using the Debbugs instance at [[https://bugs.gnu.org]]; it may still be used for these purposes until December 31st, 2025, after which Codeberg will become the sole option to report new bugs and propose new changes[[#FOOT47][^{47}]]. With Debbugs, bug reports are sent by email to [[mailto:bug-guix@gnu.org][bug-guix@gnu.org]] and patches are sent to [[mailto:guix-patches@gnu.org][guix-patches@gnu.org]] (see [[id:guix--Submitting-Patches][Submitting Patches]]).
