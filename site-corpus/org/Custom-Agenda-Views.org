*** 11.6 Custom Agenda Views [[#Custom-Agenda-Views-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Custom-Agenda-Views-1
:CLASS: section
:END:
Custom agenda commands serve two purposes: to store and quickly access frequently used TODO and tags searches, and to create special composite agenda buffers. Custom agenda commands are accessible through the dispatcher (see [[id:org--Agenda-Dispatcher][The Agenda Dispatcher]]), just like the default commands.
