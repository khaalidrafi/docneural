*** 12.9 Horizontal Rules [[#Horizontal-Rules-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Horizontal-Rules-1
:CLASS: section

A line consisting of only dashes, and at least 5 of them, is exported as a horizontal line.
