**** 13.12.9.2 MathML and OpenDocument formula files [[#MathML-and-OpenDocument-formula-files-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: MathML-and-OpenDocument-formula-files-1
:CLASS: subsubsection
:END:
When embedding LaTeX math snippets in ODT documents is not reliable, there is one more option to try. Embed an equation by linking to its MathML (‘=.mml=') source or its OpenDocument formula (‘=.odf=') file as shown below:

#+begin_example
[[./equation.mml]]
#+end_example

or

#+begin_example
[[./equation.odf]]
#+end_example
