*** 13.12 OpenDocument Text Export [[#OpenDocument-Text-Export-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: OpenDocument-Text-Export-1
:CLASS: section
:END:
The ODT export backend handles creating of OpenDocument Text (ODT) format. Documents created by this exporter use the OpenDocument-v1.2 specification[[#FOOT145][^{145}]] and are compatible with LibreOffice 3.4.
