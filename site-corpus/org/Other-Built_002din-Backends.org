*** 13.16 Other Built-in Backends [[#Other-Built_002din-Backends-1][¶]]
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: Other-Built_002din-Backends-1
:CLASS: section
:END:
Other export backends included with Org are:

- ‘=ox-man.el=': Export to a man page.

To activate such backends, either customize =org-export-backends= or load directly with ‘=(require 'ox-man)='. On successful load, the backend adds new keys in the export dispatcher (see [[id:org--The-Export-Dispatcher][The Export Dispatcher]]).

Follow the comment section of such files, for example, ‘=ox-man.el=', for usage and configuration details.
